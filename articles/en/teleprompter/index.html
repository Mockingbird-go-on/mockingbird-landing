A Teleprompter for Interviews: What It Is and How It Works

Technology2026-09-25·4 min

In short: an interview teleprompter is a program that recognizes the interviewer's questions and shows answer text on screen in real time, like a TV prompter. Unlike a classic teleprompter, the text isn't written in advance — a language model generates it for the specific question.

How it differs from a classic teleprompter

A teleprompter shows a pre-written script. An AI teleprompter for interviews generates the answer on the fly: it hears the question, searches your résumé for context and writes the answer. Preparing a script in advance is impossible for an interview — questions are unpredictable.

What it looks like in action

The answer window is usually placed on a second monitor or on top of the call. The text appears gradually (streaming) while you speak. In Invisible Mode the window never gets into screen sharing or recordings.

Why it's called an “assistant”, not a “prompter”

Because the task is not to dictate for you but to help recall and structure. You read the meaning and say it in your own words. This keeps the answer natural.

Who it suits

  • Candidates for technical and analytical positions.
  • People who get very nervous and lose the structure of answers.
  • Specialists who need to refresh a wide tech stack.

FAQ

Is it the same as a prompter?

Close in meaning, but a classic prompter shows pre-written text while an AI teleprompter generates the answer in real time for the specific question.

Can I type answers in advance?

You can, but it misses the point: interview questions are unpredictable. Auto-generation based on your résumé works better.

Is the teleprompter visible during screen sharing?

Depends on the program. Mockingbird has Invisible Mode that excludes the window from screen capture.
